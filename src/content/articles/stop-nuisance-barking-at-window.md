Window barking is one of the most self-reinforcing behaviors a dog can develop: from your dog's perspective, they bark at the mail carrier, the mail carrier eventually leaves — meaning barking "worked" every single time. Breaking that cycle needs both management and training.

## Step-by-Step Plan

1. **Reduce visual access first.** Window film, furniture rearranged away from the window, or closing blinds during peak triggers (mail delivery time) removes the opportunity to rehearse the behavior — the single highest-leverage change for many dogs.
2. **Teach an alternative response to triggers.** Train a "go to mat" cue paired with a reward, so your dog has a specific, well-reinforced job to do when something passes by instead of rushing the window.
3. **Counter-condition the common triggers.** Pair the sight of passersby, delivery vehicles, or other dogs with high-value treats delivered calmly, building a new association over repetition.
4. **Interrupt early, before full escalation.** Catching the behavior at the first look or low growl and redirecting is far easier than interrupting once your dog is in a full barking frenzy.
5. **Increase overall exercise and enrichment.** A dog with unmet activity needs is often more reactive to every passing trigger — addressing the baseline energy level can reduce the intensity of the response.

## Common Mistakes

- Yelling "quiet," which can sound like joining in and may increase arousal rather than reduce it.
- Leaving full visual access to a busy street unaddressed while trying to train through it — management makes training dramatically easier.
- Only working on it occasionally, when consistency is what actually breaks a well-established, self-reinforcing habit.

## Realistic Expectations

Because window barking is usually deeply reinforced by its own "success," expect several weeks of consistent management and training before you see a real reduction. If it doesn't improve with consistent effort, a structured training program can help identify what's maintaining the behavior and build a more targeted plan.
